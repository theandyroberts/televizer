QUOTE MODE

A little text to lift

Good presentations do not need to repeat every word on the page. They need to find the sentence that carries the idea, give it room, and let the audience understand why it matters.

Televizer keeps the source visible while a selected phrase moves into focus. Select a few words, release at the end of the swipe, and leave the pointer still for the quote to appear.
